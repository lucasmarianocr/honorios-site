Artist's profile

About Honorio's Art

Born and raised in Buenos Aires, Honorio's affection for art began early in his life.

During his studies, both at school and in the university, he felt most attached to the activities that involved precise drawing.

Later, he found his passion in nature, perfecting his technique while studying the stance, movement, gaze, and habitat of his main characters. He quickly discovered how challenging — yet inspiring — portraying wildlife can be. Each new species turned out to be more demanding than the last; honoring any of them took up to several months to complete a single piece.

By 2008, Buenos Aires's artistic scene began acknowledging Honorio's Art, including his work in a yearly published dossier.

He is currently working on several projects, and his art can be found in many international art fairs.

Process

Months in the making

Honoring a subject is a slow act. Each species demands its own study — the way light sits on fur, the tension in a gaze, the specific weight of a paw. What starts as reference becomes months of layered oil, built until the canvas holds still the way the animal never could.

Currently, Honorio is working on several new pieces, with completed works shown regularly at international art fairs.
